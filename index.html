Associate Professor · Department of Artificial Intelligence

Yeo-Chan Yoon

윤여찬 · Jeju National University, Republic of Korea

I lead research at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Multimodal Deep Learning, and Large Language Models. Before joining Jeju National University, I spent 15 years as a Principal Researcher at ETRI. My recent work spans conversational recommendation, aspect-based sentiment analysis, instruction alignment, and robust multimodal emotion recognition.

Research Interests

💬

Natural Language Processing

Conversational recommendation, sentiment analysis, dependency parsing, document clustering, and instruction alignment for language models.

🎛️

Multimodal Deep Learning

Cross-modal translation, visual dialog, image captioning, and emotion recognition that fuses voice, text, and vision.

🧠

Large Language Models

Retrieval-augmented generation, collaborative embedding-infused LLMs, and robust, role-aware transformer architectures.
